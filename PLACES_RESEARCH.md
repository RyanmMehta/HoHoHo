# Holiday activity research

Checked October 29, 2026. NYC outings for the November–December 2026 planning season. Venue facts come from official operators; home ideas are original suggestions. Selection expresses interest, not a reservation. Prices are intentionally not fixed because sessions and rentals vary. Evening availability remains 7 p.m.–midnight New York time, but early-closing venues are explicitly flagged.

## Angelina Paris
Bryant Park · 1050 Sixth Avenue

Rich hot chocolate and French pastries. Walk-in only. Closes 8 p.m. weekdays and 7 p.m. weekends: plan an early stop.

Source: https://angelinaparisusa.com/pages/locations

## MarieBelle Cacao Bar
SoHo

A cozy chocolate-and-dessert stop. Check current table availability and closing time before choosing an evening.

Source: https://mariebelle.com/pages/cacao-bar

## Bryant Park ice skating
Midtown · Winter Village

Skate in the park, then browse the holiday stalls. Reserve a skating session. Admission is free; skate rentals cost extra.

Source: https://bryantpark.org/activities/the-rink

## StuyTown ice skating
Stuyvesant Town

A neighborhood rink for a smaller outing. Access check needed: StuyTown describes resident access; rink rules require students to be residents or guests of a resident. Confirm group eligibility first.

Source: https://www.iceatstuytown.com/pricing-hours

## Wollman Rink
Central Park

Open-air skating with the skyline around you. 2026–27 season is listed. Book a session and check rental costs.

Source: https://wollmanrinknyc.com/plan/

## The Rink at Rockefeller Center
Rockefeller Center

The classic Midtown holiday skating outing. Timed tickets required; check session availability and rental details.

Source: https://www.rockefellercenter.com/tickets/the-rink-at-rockefeller-center/

## Bryant Park Holiday Shops
Midtown · Winter Village

Browse gifts and share a few market snacks. Shop hours differ from rink hours; check closing time before a 7 p.m. start.

Source: https://bryantpark.org/shop-eat/holiday-shops

## Union Square Holiday Market
Union Square

A downtown gift-and-snack wander. Listed Nov 12–Dec 24. Thu–Sat closes 9 p.m.; Sunday 8 p.m. Recheck holiday hours.

Source: https://urbanspacemarkets.com/pages/union-square

## Columbus Circle Holiday Market
Southwest corner of Central Park

Pair gift browsing with a park-side walk. Dec 1–31, 2026. Closes 7 p.m. Mon–Thu and 8 p.m. Fri–Sun: needs an earlier start.

Source: https://urbanspacemarkets.com/pages/columbus-circle

## Grand Central Holiday Fair
Vanderbilt Hall

An indoor artisan market for a cold or rainy day. Nov 9–Dec 24, 2026. Earlier outing recommended; official page has differing hours, so confirm before going.

Source: https://grandcentralterminal.com/holiday-fair/

## Rockefeller Christmas tree walk
Rockefeller Plaza

Tree photos and a Midtown winter stroll. The 2026 tree lighting is Dec 2. Plan your illuminated-tree visit after that; expect crowds.

Source: https://www.rockefellercenter.com/holidays/rockefeller-center-christmas-tree/

## Dyker Heights lights walk
Dyker Heights · Brooklyn

Explore the neighborhood’s decorated homes. Private residential displays vary; no guaranteed schedule. Keep sidewalks clear and respect residents. Allow extra travel time.

Source: https://www.nyc.gov/assets/cecm/downloads/pdf/dos-and-donts-dyker-heights.pdf

## Bronx Zoo Holiday Lights
Bronx Zoo

Animal lanterns and illuminated trails. Select dates Nov 21, 2026–Jan 3, 2027. Lights start 4:30 p.m.; closes 9 p.m., last entry 8 p.m.

Source: https://bronxzoo.com/holiday-lights/faqs

## Christmas Spectacular
Radio City Music Hall

A festive show with the Radio City Rockettes. 2026 performances start Nov 4. Choose an evening performance and check seat prices together.

Source: https://www.rockettes.com/christmas/

## Holiday Train Show
New York Botanical Garden · Bronx

Miniature NYC landmarks and model trains in the conservatory. Nov 14, 2026–Jan 10, 2027. Choose one of the extended evening dates; not every night runs late.

Source: https://www.nybg.org/event/holiday-train-show-2026

## Research decisions
- StuyTown is selectable as conditional interest, not promised public admission. Its resident FAQ and rink student eligibility rules require an access check. https://www.stuytown.com/faqs
- Grand Central has inconsistent hours within its official page; do not promise late opening.
- Angelina closes by 7 p.m. on weekends, so it cannot fill a 7 p.m.–midnight block without an earlier start.
- Union Square source lists month/day dates without an explicit season year in that section; recheck before booking.
- Excluded Max Brenner Union Square because current closure reports conflict with older venue listings; no reliable operating confirmation established.
- No automatic bookings, ticket purchases, or calendar writes occur when voting.
